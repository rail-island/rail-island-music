| File | Origin | Author | Licence | Use |

| `Dans_l_antre_du_roi_de_la_montagne.ly` | Mutopia Project, `ftp/GriegE/O46/Dans_l_antre_du_roi_de_la_montagne/` in https://github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject (Grieg's piano arrangement, The University Society, 1918) | Edvard Grieg; typeset by Coyau | Public Domain (Mutopia) | the theme, its answer in F-sharp, the coda |

`score.abc` is our own reduction: the theme and its answer as one melody with
chord symbols, in seven sections that climb in register and speed up from
112 to 176 beats per minute (the original's accelerando), and a short coda.
The doublings and the stretto chords of the piano version are left out.
