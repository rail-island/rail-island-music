|---|---|---|---|---|
| `EliteSyncopations.ly` | Mutopia Project, `ftp/JoplinS/EliteSyncopations/` in https://github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject | Scott Joplin; typeset for Mutopia | Public Domain (Mutopia) | the melody and the left-hand harmony |

`score.abc` is our own reduction: the top line of the right hand as the melody
and the left hand's harmony as chord symbols; intro, the four strains and all
repeats kept. Octave doublings and inner voices are left out.
