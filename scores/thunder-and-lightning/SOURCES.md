| `blitz.ly` | Mutopia Project, `ftp/StraussJJ/O324/blitz/` in https://github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject (C. M. Cady, 1878) | Johann Strauss II; typeset by Stan Sanderson | Public Domain (Mutopia) | the melody of the polka, the second strain and the trio |

`score.abc` is our own reduction: the top line of the right hand as the
melody, in the form polka - second strain - trio - polka - second strain. The
chord symbols are reconstructed from the chords of the right hand and are
simpler than Strauss's harmony; the introduction and the second half of the
trio are left out.
