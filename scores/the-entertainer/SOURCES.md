# The Entertainer - sources

| File | Origin | Author | Licence | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `entertainer.ly` | Mutopia Project, `ftp/JoplinS/entertainer/` in https://github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject (from the original 1902 edition) | Scott Joplin; typeset by Chris Sawer, revised by Simon Albrecht | Public Domain (Mutopia) | the melody and the left-hand harmony |

`score.abc` is our own reduction: the top line of the right hand as the melody
and the left hand's harmony as chord symbols; intro, the four strains, the
interlude and all repeats kept. The right hand's octave doublings and inner
voices are left out.
