# Maple Leaf Rag - sources

| File | Origin | Author | Licence | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `maple.ly` | Mutopia Project, `ftp/JoplinS/maple/` in https://github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject | Scott Joplin; typeset for Mutopia | Public Domain (Mutopia) | the melody and the left-hand harmony |

`score.abc` is our own reduction: the top line of the right hand as the melody
and the left hand's harmony as chord symbols, in the form AABBACCDD. The right
hand's octave doublings and inner voices are left out; some octave placements
follow the edition's relative notation and may differ from the print by an
octave.
