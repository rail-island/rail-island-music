# Jingle 3 - sources

- `recording.mid` - the user's own piano recording `Recording_2026-11-01_11_56.mid`
  (played live, 60 bpm nominal, millisecond timing, sustain pedal). The motif is
  the user's; nothing else was used.

Both scores were transcribed by hand from this recording, read note by note from
its onsets, velocities and pedal (see docs/music/jingles.md):

- `tidy.abc` - the recording as played, D minor: an A/F-E-D-C-D fall over a D pedal in octaves. Quantized to eighths on
  a steady tempo, the stray and zero-length touches dropped, the pedalled bass
  written as notes of their length, the melody kept.
- `rewrite.abc` - the same idea written again cleanly: adds a Gm-A7 cadence and a closing D minor chord.
