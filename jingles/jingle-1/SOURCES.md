- `recording.mid` - the user's own piano recording `Recording_2026-11-01_11_53.mid`
  (played live, 60 bpm nominal, millisecond timing, sustain pedal). The motif is
  the user's; nothing else was used.

Both scores were transcribed by hand from this recording, read note by note from
its onsets, velocities and pedal (see docs/music/jingles.md):

- `tidy.abc` - the recording as played, D major: a falling F#-E-D-A motif over a bass descending D, C#, C, B (D, A/C#, D7/C, G/B). The recording rushes bars 2-3; tidy evens them to whole bars. Quantized to eighths on
  a steady tempo, the stray and zero-length touches dropped, the pedalled bass
  written as notes of their length, the melody kept.
- `rewrite.abc` - the same idea written again cleanly: adds a G minor colour and an A7 turn so it resolves to D.
